# Surface: landing + stage shell (index.html)

Mode: Experience (the visitor is inside the work; the landing is its first room, not a marketing page). Audience: the professor, alone on a laptop after a one-minute intro. Job: open a case. Constraints: Arabic RTL, offline-capable, reduced motion, keyboard.

## Direction contract
THESIS: A ledger page being ruled, not a hero. The page IS the first folio of the court's register; it refuses the hero/headline/CTA/feature-cards arrangement.
OWN-WORLD: Mint accounting paper #E3EBD8 with real ledger columns (البيان | مدين | دائن | الرصيد | الإحالة) in rule green #7FA58E, blue-black ink #1F3B4D, pencil grey #6E6E68 for the visitor, eraser pink #E9B8B0 for corrections, closing red #C2272D only for double rules, strikes and the verdict. Aref Ruqaa as the clerk's hand; Noto Naskh body; IBM Plex Sans Arabic only for tabular figures (chosen because the data must align in columns with Western digits; nothing else on the page uses it). No cards, no icons from libraries; pen strokes, pin holes, folds.
STORY: The professor sees a register being opened, reads the court's question «من يحاكم الأرقام؟», understands five real cases are on the docket with their real auditors, and opens one.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed ruled folio. Right margin column «الإحالة» holds folio number and date. Title in Aref Ruqaa written by the pen across the البيان column at ~14vw. Below, the docket: five entries written as ledger lines (company · sector · auditor · FY · a red marginal note where the auditor qualified or doubted), each line is the action. Secondary line «قدّم قضية جديدة» in pencil (upload arrives in Plan 3). Bottom margin: «كيف تأكدنا» and «ما لا يستطيع النموذج قوله».
FORM: ledger-register docket, list position 1 of my ordered list. seed: none (pinned) — student chose «ورق الأستاذ»: "ورق الأستاذ".
Signature interaction: the pen rules the page and writes the title; choosing a docket line draws the red double rule under it and folds the page into the case envelope.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
